## Mission

We help small and mid-sized stone fruit and berry growers on the southern
plains stay in business by sharing research, training, and market access.

## Populations served

- About 340 member growers per year, operating under 50 acres of specialty
  crops each. Membership is open to any grower in the three-state service area.

## Programs

- **Late-Frost Network:** a grower-run network of 42 orchard weather stations
  with text alerts when bloom-stage frost is forecast. 310 growers enrolled in
  2025. Source: 2025 annual report.
- **Packhouse Food Safety Coaching:** one-on-one coaching toward a third-party
  produce safety audit. 58 growers coached since 2022; 41 passed their first
  audit. Source: program records.
- **Plains Fruit Market Days:** a seasonal wholesale buyer-grower meetup held
  in two cities. 2025 sales recorded at the event: $212,000. Source: buyer
  survey.
