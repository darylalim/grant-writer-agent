## Identity

- **Legal name:** Three Rivers Rural Health District
- **Entity type:** Multi-county local health department
- **UEI (SAM.gov):** UNKNOWN
- **Headquarters:** Calder
- **Service area:** Four counties, 92% rural, total population about 61,000

## Mission

We protect the health of everyone in our four counties, especially the people
the health system reaches last.

## Programs

- **Communicable disease and immunization clinic** in each county seat. Tested
  1,140 people for HIV and 980 for hepatitis C in 2025; 9 new hepatitis C
  diagnoses and 2 new HIV diagnoses.
- **Community health workers:** 4 CHWs since 2023, each living in the county
  they serve. 3,600 contacts and 410 completed referrals in 2025.
- **Syringe services program**, run with Harbor Recovery Collective (a peer
  recovery nonprofit) under state authorization since 2022, at two fixed sites
  and one mobile route.
- **PrEP:** offered at the clinics since 2024; 31 people prescribed in 2025.

## Partners

- **State Public Health Laboratory** has run SARS-CoV-2 wastewater testing for
  the district's two largest treatment plants (Calder and Marrow Falls) since
  2022, twice weekly. Letter of commitment: verbal; not yet in writing.
- **Calder Municipal Water Reclamation** serves about 14,000 residents.
- **Marrow Falls Wastewater Treatment Plant** serves about 6,500 residents.
- **Harbor Recovery Collective** (syringe services, peer navigation).
- **Northfork Community Health Center**, the nearest FQHC, which accepts
  linkage referrals for HIV and hepatitis C treatment.
